  • Hourly/Daily Guard (site_bakim.py · damga_bayat): Every measured item carries a son_olcum + 14 days validity window. Expired items trigger a damga: K0xx vadesi geçti alert.

Which items are included in the Benefit Ledger table and which are excluded?
Out of 75 total inventory items, only the 49 measured items backed by a primary file, log, or self-test script appear in the table. The 14 items awaiting measurement and 12 unverified items are reported strictly as aggregate counts and excluded by name.
How does the 14-day validity and archive gate work?
Each item has a 14-day validity deadline (2026-11-04) from its last measurement timestamp. The site_bakim.py guard checks all deadlines via the damga_bayat gate and archives unmeasured items without deletion.
How are the primary evidence links verified?
