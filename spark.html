Spark · producing theses

How the studio produces theses

Spark is not a chat; when the round ends, a scorecard is written.

From day one, the project belongs to Atlas (Gemini 4 Argon · 1M context) as chief architect and orchestrator. Spark (Kıvılcım in Turkish) is our procedure — led at the very top by Orchestra Atlas (Argon 1M), together with the context-free Argon Web external seat, the Vizier gate (Anu · Claude Fable), Opus on project sessions and architectural code, Sonnet on production code, and Haiku on mechanical measurement — for producing theses and theories from blind voices that cannot hear each other, and subjecting them to Astra and Grok’s counter-thesis and Red Team stress tests. Testing a ready-made verdict is a separate job; that is called a referee round, and the two are not mixed.

This page has three parts: the seven-step procedure, the scorecard of the rounds so far, and three real examples of the procedure. The procedure is dated 21 Sep 2026 (architectural update: 14–16 Oct 2026).

Seven steps

Run in order. The round starts before the brief is sent and does not end when the answers arrive.

  1. Pre-expectation (Chief Architect & Orchestra Atlas · Gemini 4 Argon)

    From the very beginning, the project is Atlas’s. Before the round starts, at the top Orchestra Atlas (Gemini 4 Argon · 1M context) writes the answer it expects in a single line and seals it to the scorecard file through the Vizier gate. No brief is sent until it is written.

    The aim is to measure honestly, afterwards, whether the round changed anything.

  2. Measure

    The STATE section of the brief is built from measured items. What cannot be measured is written as “UNKNOWN”.

  3. Brief (anonymous)

    In production mode no verdict is asked for. Every voice is asked for the same form:

    • Thesis (one sentence)
    • →
    • Theory (mechanism)
    • →
    • Source (author · year · venue or address; if none, “NO SOURCE”)
    • →
    • Counter-thesis
    • →
    • Falls (the measurement that would kill the thesis)

    No line cap is set; the answer is not reduced to a checklist.

  4. Voices, Gates, Context-Free Argon Web and Red Team

    Led at the top by Atlas (Gemini 4 Argon · 1M context), the blind channels and gates include: the Context-Free Argon Web seat (isolated from session history), the Vizier gate (Anu · Claude Fable), Opus on project sessions and architectural code, Sonnet on production code, Haiku on mechanical measurement, Astra (frequently serving as counter-thesis and Red Team stress-tester), Grok Bot / Grok Assistant for outside pulse and counter-thesis, local models (Gemma 4 / Qwen), and the inner voices (Enki and Enlil). In Wave 1 none sees another’s answer; in Wave 2 Astra and Grok subject the theses to Red Team refutation.

  5. Identity, Vizier Gate and source verification

    No number is taken from a model whose identity has not been confirmed. Every source the voices give must pass the Vizier gate and verification sieve before entering synthesis; a citation that cannot be found stays stamped “UNVERIFIED” and is not used as support.

    “The brightest reference is the one most often made up.”

  6. Top Synthesis (Atlas · Gemini 4 Argon)

    At the top, Atlas (Gemini 4 Argon · 1M) collides the verified evidence coming through the Vizier gate (Fable), the Context-Free Argon Web voice, and project sessions (Opus) against Astra / Grok’s counter-theses: agreed · split (the decision is there) · theses and theories (whose, with which source) · recommendation · kill test. The raw answers are kept.

    On a deep topic a second round opens: the measurement in between is run, then a second brief goes out.

  7. Scorecard

    When the round ends, “did the decision change” is written on the same line as the pre-expectation: YES | SHARPENED | NO, plus a one-line note.

Agent evolution: no new agent, the rule set evolves

The EVOLUTION-2 Spark · 7 Oct 2026.

Spark source

The brief went out in production mode, anonymous. The question: what should the second evolution of the family of three agents (Enki · Enlil · Enkidu) be? The state was written as measured:

  • Enki says “no verdict” in most rounds.
  • Enlil is the blind auditor: 24,990 records, 1,521 suspect, work produced 0. The one tool it wrote itself is awaiting approval.
  • Enkidu runs 9 mechanical tasks.
  • The dynamism line: verdict → kill test → the next tick measures. The tests are underpowered (n is small).

Four voices wrote: Gemini (no context, two seats), Gemma (local) and Grok Bot. They did not hear each other.

The pre-expectation was written before the round: no new agent; a proposal to attach “friction” to Enlil will come and will be rejected; the blind mutation engine will be Enkidu.

Counter-thesis

The voices split in four places; the decision was there. The hardest counter-thesis was the manual alternative: if the owner looked at the list of decaying rules for 15 minutes once a week, the same result might come out.

Split · who produces the mutation

Proposal

“100 local variations a day”. The zero-cost assumption collapsed the same day, under GPU starvation.

Counter

“One a day, ≤3 candidates + placebo 4:1”.

Decision

Placebo was chosen.

Split · scale

Proposal

Three steps in 30 days. That was the 30-day plan of three voices.

Counter

The arithmetic: at a 76% base rate, seeing a 10-point difference with 80% power takes ≈240 episodes per arm. Three steps do not fit into 30 days.

Decision

30 days = one step. The plan of the three voices was optimistic.

Split · the manual alternative

Proposal

The automatic loop: mutation → selection → consolidation.

Counter

A look by hand: if the owner looked at the list of decaying rules for 15 minutes once a week, the same result might come out.

Decision

Accepted as the kill test: two weeks in parallel; if they are equal, the automation stops.

Split · contrast embedding analysis

Proposal

“Contrast embedding analysis”.

Counter

An experiment the same day: the embedding model saw contrary verdicts as 0.90 similar.

Decision

Removed.

Synthesis

All four of the four voices agreed on these (4/4):

  • No new agent is written. What evolves is the rule set and the harness (genotype = rule, phenotype = harness).
  • The loop: mutation → selection (kill test + Wilson interval/MDE) → consolidation. Automatic into shadow; into the real system only with the owner’s approval.
  • Enlil stays blind; selection gets a blind-judge gate.
  • The main measure is the number of closed loops: if it does not rise in four weeks, there is no evolution.

Source verification: two academic citations were verified on the web; one had been given with the wrong year (2024 instead of 2026). One citation could not be verified and was not used as support.

Kill test

Two measurements kill the thesis. The first is the manual alternative: it runs in parallel for two weeks, and if the two are equal the automation stops. The second is the main measure: if the number of closed loops does not rise in four weeks, there is no evolution.

Not adopted

  • “100 local variations a day”. The zero-cost assumption collapsed the same day, under GPU starvation.
  • The three-step 30-day plan. It did not fit the arithmetic of ≈240 episodes per arm.
  • “Contrast embedding analysis”. Refuted by experiment the same day; removed.
  • The unverifiable citation. Not used as support.

Order

The synthesis turned into an order: EVOLUTION-2 Step 1, in shadow.

  • Enkidu decaying list + placebo
  • →
  • Enki ≤3 candidates
  • →
  • Enlil blind stamp
  • →
  • Enkidu shadow statistics gate (240 episodes/arm)
  • →
  • Consolidation

It was built the same day: 7 tools, 153 tests. The model that built it left 9 objections; the objections went on record. One of them: “the backward arm, at a 24-hour horizon, takes ~218 days to reach 240 episodes → a structural zero in this arm”.

Scorecard

SHARPENED. The expectation held; the new parts that sharpened it: the placebo, the 240-episode arithmetic and the manual alternative as kill test.
