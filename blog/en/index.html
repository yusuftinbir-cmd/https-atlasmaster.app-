Atlas Studio · Project Log

The studio's working log: we discuss topics chosen from the news with different AI models, and write what we recommend and what data would change our mind.

NOTICE · 11 October 2026

So far the articles here have only used the Spark algorithm: thesis, counter-thesis, source verification, synthesis. We have not yet written an article that produces a real spark and renders a verdict. We will.

Topics chosen from the news are worked through a Spark round: the same question is asked of different AI models that can't see one another's answers; the sources they give are checked one by one, and where they agree and diverge is written up.

Every post has three things: method, references and the data that would change our mind.
