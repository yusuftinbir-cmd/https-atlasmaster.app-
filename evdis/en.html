🏥 Operating Room & General Anesthesia

Surgical & Dental Care in a Full Operating Room

Advanced maxillofacial surgeries and comprehensive pediatric or adult treatments can be performed under general anesthesia and conscious sedation in a fully equipped operating room led by Dr. Dt. Seyit Yılmaz and Uzm. Dr. Gökhan Gedikli.

🔬 High-Resolution Operating Microscope

Endodontic, Restorative & Aesthetic Micro-Dentistry

In endodontics (root canal therapy & retreatment) and restorative/aesthetic dentistry, our electronic operating microscope reveals hidden micro-canals and margins invisible to the naked eye.

🤖 AI-Assisted Radiology & Diagnostics

AI Film Analysis on Panoramic & 3D CBCT Scans

Our panoramic X-ray and 3D CBCT workflow integrates AI-assisted radiographic image analysis to map nerve canals, sinuses, roots, and bone volume—supporting faster, high-precision clinical diagnosis.

⚙️ CEREC & 3D CAD/CAM Milling

Tray-Free 3D Optical Scanning & In-Clinic CAD/CAM

Instead of traditional impression trays, CEREC 3D intraoral optical scanners capture digital impressions in seconds and mill ceramic inlays, onlays, and crowns with micron accuracy.

Information

Frequently asked questions

How are X-rays and treatment planning done on the first visit?

Both branches offer digital panoramic X-ray and our Serdivan branch features 3D dental tomography (CBCT), allowing treatment planning to be shared on the same day in most cases.

How are sedation and general anesthesia procedures conducted?

At our Serdivan branch, sedation and general anesthesia are performed for eligible pediatric and adult patients under the evaluation and supervision of Specialist Anesthesiologist Dr. Gökhan Gedikli.
