1. Digital smile design and laboratory workflow in Sakarya (Serdivan & Adapazarı)

Developed within the Sakarya (Serdivan and Adapazarı) digital CAD/CAM and laboratory ecosystem, the AtlasMaster workflow separates smile design into three measurable layers:

  • Photo-based Constrained Canvas preview (2D): Operating on a de-identified mouth crop with a 4,686-pixel lip lock (contour deviation p95 ≤ 0.2 mm), the facial midline, incisal curve, and gingival zeniths are locked so photorealistic previews preserve true anatomical contours before any bur touches the tooth.
  • 478-point 3D cephalometric facial analysis: From MediaPipe FaceMesh 3D coordinates, bizygomatic width (ZY-ZY, #234–#454) and bigonial mandibular width (Go-Go, #172–#397) are measured against anthropometric thresholds (Farkas 1994; Williams 1914; Frush & Fisher 1958 SPA factor; Lombardi 1973).
  • 441-form tooth library and ExoCAD package (3D): Central incisor width-to-height ratio is segmented via SAM 2D to select 5 priority and 3 alternative morphologies from a 441-form library (476/477 STL/PLY 3D renders) for direct ExoCAD and chairside CAD/CAM fabrication.

3. Visual preview before tooth preparation and joint decision with the dentist

The engineering purpose of digital smile design preview is conservative planning and expectation alignment. Combining a 2D/3D Constrained Canvas preview with an intraoral temporary trial (mock-up) delivers three practical benefits:

  • Enamel preservation: Through digital design and temporary mock-up before a bur touches the tooth, dentist and individual inspect the target morphology, supporting silicone-index composite injection (injection moulding) or prepless veneers without unnecessary reduction.
  • Resolving natural vs prominent aesthetics upfront: Reviewing the photo preview and 3D STL/PLY design together reduces laboratory remakes across Serdivan and Adapazarı workflows.
  • CAD/CAM material alignment: Glass-ceramic, composite moulding, and multi-layered monolithic zirconia restorations follow the same 3D digital blueprint.

4. Frequently asked questions (Smile design Sakarya)

How does photo-based 2D and 3D smile design preview work in Sakarya?
