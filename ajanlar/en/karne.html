Method

Each agent's self-test command is executed in a separate subprocess and the final verification token and gate counts are recorded. P0 is the highest priority label used by the readers and maintenance members; the P0 figure here counts ledger rows accumulated over time, including later-resolved findings, and is not a count of open violations. The preparation date is when the page was generated; the moment it went live is recorded separately in the ledger as a 'yayinlandi' (published) row once the live page fingerprint is verified. Because the reader and maintenance ledgers are append-only and idempotent, the ledger row and P0 columns represent cumulative totals rather than point-in-time open counts. Agents that do not write to a ledger or tokens that do not carry gate counts are marked with '—' without fabricating unmeasured numbers.
