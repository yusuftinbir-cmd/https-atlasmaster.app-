Reaper — auditor agent: source verification method and scorecard

✓ Verified by Reaper · 360/394 · 19 Oct 2026

Reaper carries out two jobs together: (1) in multi-voice Spark (Kıvılcım) rounds, testing every academic and regulatory citation one by one against Crossref, PubMed E-utils, and DOI registries to eliminate fabricated references and generic textbook summaries; (2) checking with a proof command whether every method named on this site and in its documents is actually running today (1 round every 6 hours). Spark stays in the middle; Reaper runs in its own isolated context and is itself audited via deliberate fault injection.

Method: source verification and rote-summary filtering

In a multi-voice Spark round, blind external and local voices submit a thesis, theory, and bibliography for each question without hearing one another. Before synthesis, Reaper tests every citation one by one against primary registries (Crossref API, PubMed E-utils, doi.org, WHO, and Official Gazette archives):

  • One-by-one citation verification (Crossref / PubMed / DOI): First author, publication year (±1), journal or publisher, and title core are queried against primary records. Matching records are stamped DOĞRULANDI (verified); vague citations that cannot be located are stamped DOĞRULANAMADI (unverified) and cannot be used as support.
  • A fabricated citation invalidates the answer (light class included): A non-existent article, fake title, or author–journal mismatch is classified as UYDURMA · AĞIR (heavy fabrication); a real article paired with a non-existent DOI/PMID or an identifier pointing to a different paper is classified as UYDURMA · HAFİF (light fabrication). The rule is mechanical: any fabricated citation — light class included — eliminates that voice’s thesis on that question from synthesis and halves its weight in the next round.
  • No voice is a benchmark by authority alone: No distinction is made between a frontier cloud model or a local model; no voice is exempt from verification by virtue of its title or authority. In Spark, the measure is what proves true.
  • Rote-summary tag (EZBER): Even when a citation is real, does the voice’s output bring specific counter-evidence or a sharpening objection to the brief’s question and the author’s field thesis, or does it merely repeat a textbook / general literature summary? Generic summaries that bring neither new evidence nor a concrete objection are filtered out with the EZBER tag and do not enter synthesis.
Verification classDefinition and mechanical sanction
DOĞRULANDI (verified)Primary record found; first author + year + journal/publisher + title core match. Enters synthesis.
DOĞRULANAMADI (unverified)Record not found (journal name only or vague citation); no proof of fabrication, but excluded from synthesis support.
UYDURMA · AĞIR (heavy fabrication)Non-existent paper, non-existent DOI, author–journal mismatch, or fake title. Thesis on that question is eliminated.
UYDURMA · HAFİF (light fabrication)Paper is real, but the supplied identifier (DOI or PMID) does not exist or belongs to another paper. Counted as fabrication by mechanical rule; thesis eliminated.
EZBER (rote summary)Citation is not fabricated, but the response is a generic literature summary that brings no specific evidence or objection to the brief’s question; excluded from synthesis.

System audit: what it measures and three arms

Three things are written for every method: a proof command, the expected result and an owner. Reaper runs the command and compares the output with the expectation. The method list: methods described on the site, tools with a contract, scheduled services, runners and agents.

ArmEngineJob
MeasurementQwen 3.8 27B (local) + mechanical proofCollects the evidence; gives no verdict.
JudgementGemma 4 31B (local)Classifies each method and writes its fall test.
SynthesisArgon (high thinking)Compares the two arms, resolves conflicts by running the command itself, updates the site and the board.

Special-training plan: the untrained baseline is measured first on a blind set of 50+ methods; training is done only if the baseline is low.

Fall test

In every round Reaper runs three deliberate faults against itself (ageing live evidence by 9 days → DEAD; dead method + “automatic” claim → CLAIM; hanging command or malformed JSON → UNMEASURED); if one goes uncaught, that day’s verdict is void and the page says “DENETÇİ DÜŞTÜ” (the auditor fell).

Nobody is exempt — Yusuf included

The method list also has a row for “pending decisions and promises made”. The age (days) of each item is measured: 0–2 days CANLI, 3–7 days AKSAK, older than 7 days ÖLÜ. This page carries one line: “Yusuf · pending N · oldest M days”, with no private or money content; private and money items are counted by age only and their titles are not written.

Limits

Reaper does not write code, change code, delete or shut down services. It only measures, classifies and proposes; it writes the site and the board. Private matters, patient data and money are outside the audit scope and carry no numbers.
