1. From 2D preview to 3D ExoCAD scene: Why a standardized STL/PLY library matters

When a 2D smile preview is disconnected from the 3D CAD/CAM mesh milled in the laboratory, incisal edge length and axial inclinations drift during the intraoral mock-up. The AtlasMaster CAD/CAM workflow closes this gap through four measurable stages:

  • Cephalometric morphology selection: Bizygomatic (ZY-ZY) and bigonial (Go-Go) ratios are extracted from a 478-landmark MediaPipe FaceMesh 3D mesh; on the locked n = 32 synthetic test set, facial shape classification rose from 0.0% (0/32) to 90.6% (29/32), while jaw_width and target tooth line JSON output reached 100.0% (32/32).
  • 441 tooth forms and 537 guide profiles: Central incisor width-to-height ratio is segmented via SAM 2D to rank 5 priority and 3 alternative morphologies out of 441 unique tooth forms and 537 markdown guide profiles (19/21 showcase archetypes).
  • 476/477 standardized STL/PLY rendering pipeline: Across 442 base inputs plus 34 repaired shell variants, 476 of 477 target 3D meshes are verified with a deterministic SHA-1 camera matrix, alpha silhouette, and tooth index map (render_rgba, siluet_alfa, dis_indeks).
  • Cervical boundary (0x800) and p95 ≤ 0.2 mm fidelity gate: During 3D arch placement, cervical boundary vertices (0x800 flag) are locked and verified across 41/41 self-test checks to stay within p95 ≤ 0.2 mm geometric deviation.

3. Composite injection moulding, chairside veneers, and material alignment

Synthesized across 100+ verified citations and 20 field verdicts, the CAD/CAM design package aligns digital geometry with restorative material constraints across three axes:

  • Composite injection moulding and intraoral mock-up: A 3D-printed model from the verified STL/PLY design guides a transparent silicone index for non-prep intraoral mock-up or flowable composite injection before any enamel reduction.
  • Glass-ceramic (lithium disilicate) vs monolithic zirconia: Ultrathin (0.3–0.5 mm) or prepless laminate designs rely on hydrofluoric acid and silane adhesion in silica-based glass-ceramics, whereas high-masking or bruxism cases require MDP-primed multi-layered monolithic zirconia parameters defined early in ExoCAD.
  • Reducing laboratory remakes: Sharing identical coordinate locks between the 2D Constrained Canvas preview and the 3D STL/PLY arch reduces try-in revisions across Serdivan and Adapazarı dentist–laboratory deliveries.

4. Frequently asked questions (CAD/CAM dentistry & AI smile design)

How do digital smile design and the Sakarya CAD/CAM dentistry workflow connect 2D previews to 3D ExoCAD?
In the Sakarya (Serdivan and Adapazarı) CAD/CAM design workflow, intraoral STL/PLY scans are aligned with facial photographs. Bizygomatic and bigonial ratios from a 478-landmark 3D face mesh select 5 priority and 3 alternative morphologies out of 441 tooth forms (537 guide profiles, 476/477 verified STL/PLY renders) to produce a ready-to-mill 3D ExoCAD package.
What do the 441 tooth form library and 476/477 STL/PLY render verification ensure geometrically?
To prevent drift between a 2D AI smile design preview and 3D CAD/CAM milling or printing, every tooth shell is rendered through a calibrated camera matrix. Across 441 base forms and 537 guide records, 476 of 477 target entries are verified with a cervical boundary lock (0x800 flag) and p95 ≤ 0.2 mm contour fidelity (41/41 self-tests).

Before any bur touches enamel, a Constrained Canvas preview (4,686-px lip lock) and intraoral mock-up clarify aesthetic proportions with the dentist, separating silica-based glass-ceramic bonding protocols from MDP-primed monolithic zirconia or composite injection moulding.
