§ 01

What It Does

A — analysis

Analysis from Photos

Looks at the smile and retracted shots and measures tooth proportions, midline, incisal display, and gingival margins. Identity and the full-face photo stay on the device; only an anonymized mouth crop goes to the image model.

B — form

Form Recommendation

Ranks a 441-form dental library by measurements, profile, and library geometry, and hands you 5 hits and 3 exploration slots. Every card comes with its answer to "why this one?"

C — mockup

Guideline Mockup

The chosen form's outline and ridge lines are fitted onto the patient's photo, and the visual model stays inside that guide. A before/after slider goes to the dentist as a single file.

D — handover

ExoCAD Workflow

The accepted form and its constraints reach the technician's arrangement step as a ready work package.

§ 02

What Atlas Doesn't Do

Knowing the limit is part of the job.

01

It doesn't diagnose

It measures and suggests; diagnosis belongs to the dentist.

02

It doesn't decide

The decision is the dentist's. Atlas brings options; you make the choice.

03

It doesn't promise an outcome

The mockup is a design preview, not a commitment to a clinical result.

04

It doesn't send patient data out

Identity and the full-face photo stay on the device; only an anonymized mouth crop goes to the image model.

05

It doesn't do the ExoCAD arrangement

Arrangement stays in human hands: the dentist does it. Atlas only prepares the work package.

§ 03

How It Works

Five steps from two photos to an ExoCAD work package.

  1. Photo

    The dentist takes two photos: one smile, one retracted. That's it.

  2. Measurement

    The local measurement chain kicks in; profile and tooth proportions go on record.

  3. Form recommendation

    The form engine produces the list; the dentist picks a form or says which line they want.

  4. Guideline mockup

    The guideline mockup is generated; the dentist slides it back and forth to take a look.

  5. ExoCAD

    The approved design becomes an ExoCAD work package.

§ 04

Technology

Upper mind → agents → gates.

Atlas is a multi-agent system orchestrated by Claude: the decision and order layer is Claude, measurement and code tasks are distributed by model tier (Opus, Sonnet, Haiku), and other models for visual generation and research connect through a bridge.

Every tool carries its own self-test; every change passes a measurable gate. Library geometry is read directly from ExoCAD files.

A change that fails the gate does not go live.

How the agents are measured

Three agents work in the background: one proposes, one judges without seeing the context, one counts and measures. A rule that doesn't earn a passing grade is retired; the details and the measurements are on the Autonomy page.

§ 06

Form Engine

The form engine puts the library's geometry next to your measurements and lines up the forms that suit this patient best.

01

Recommendation Structure

Measurement and library data produce 5 hits and 3 exploration slots. Only 1 form per family is shown, so you won't meet five cousins of the same form.

02

Profile Only

If tooth proportions can't be measured or no preference is given, the system runs on a profile-only marker and asks the dentist instead of guessing.

03

Root-Excluded Crown Ratio

Measurements are matched to gallery distributions as percentiles. The root-excluded crown ratio steers the form choice; that's where the fine-tuning comes from.

§ 07

§ 08

Method

At Atlas, autonomous agents make the decisions, but every decision has to be measurable and testable. "Trust me" doesn't count as currency here.

01

Order and Gate

Every task comes as an order in machine language, with a set scope and a single-token verification gate. Work that can't pass the gate doesn't go live; no shortcuts.

02

Measurement First

Every change and every claim is written as a measurable hypothesis. Measure first; a claim only stands because of that measurement.

03

Spark Rounds

For hard decisions, independent models are asked for a thesis, a theory, a counter-thesis, and a falsification test. In synthesis, sources get verified and prior expectations get tested too. Spark page →

04

Agent Evolution

Agent rules are pitted against placebos in a blind-judge system; changes that get approved go in as weekly packages.

§ 09

Frequently Asked Questions

Q01

Where is patient data stored?

Identity and the full-face photo stay on the device; only an anonymized mouth crop goes to the image model.

Q02

Which models does Atlas use?

Claude runs the decision and order layer; measurement and code work is split up by model tier. Different models for research and image generation work together in a multi-agent system.

Q03

Is ExoCAD required?

No. Form recommendation and the mockup work without ExoCAD; when it comes to the arrangement step, an ExoCAD work package is prepared.

Q04

What does the dentist do?

Takes the smile and retracted photos, picks one of Atlas's suggested forms or states a line preference, then looks over the guided mockup.

Q05

What does the technician do?

Receives the work package with the dentist-approved form and constraints such as thickness limits, and carries out the arrangement step of the design.

Q06

Is this a product?

It isn't software for sale; it's the design workflow the studio uses itself. Write to us about a case.
