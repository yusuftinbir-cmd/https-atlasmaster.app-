Yusuf Aziz

Yusuf Aziz (Tınbır) is a dental CAD/CAM specialist and is currently working at Ev Diş Oral and Dental Health Center in Sakarya → evdis.com.tr

1. CAD/CAM

In digital dentistry the work runs from scan to production: intraoral scanning, model preparation, design, 3D printing and milling. The measure of the job is the part that seats in the mouth; a design is verified on the patient, not on the screen.

2. Smile design

From face to tooth: a tooth form specific to the patient, derived from facial proportions, the lip line and 3D cephalometry. A measured face instead of a ready-made template; first a visual preview, then a trial and production → Digital smile design: method and measurement

3. What AI agents are actually good for

There is a single research question: what are AI agents actually good for in a workshop and in a clinic? This site is the lab notebook for that question. Models that cannot hear each other answer the same question, work that does not pass the evidence gate does not count, and a number that is not measured is not written. What is sought is not a demonstration but fewer repetitions, fewer errors and more honest measurement in daily work → Autonomy architecture · Spark protocol · Reaper citation verification
