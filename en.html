§ 01

How it works

Five steps. What we need from you: photos, a choice and an approval.

  1. Photo

    Two photos are used: a full-face smile shot and a retracted shot. A phone camera is enough.

  2. Measurement

    Tooth proportions, midline, incisal edge and gingival line are measured from the photos.

  3. Form recommendation

    You receive a list of forms. You pick one or tell us the line you want.

  4. Guide-lined preview

    A guide-lined preview is prepared with your chosen form; you compare before/after with the slider.

  5. ExoCAD

    The design you approve becomes an ExoCAD work package.

Not a real patient. 100% synthetic face · algorithmic morphology simulation, not diagnosis/treatment/medical device. Clinical decisions belong solely to the dentist. Single synthetic example demonstration (n = 1); results may differ on other faces.

§ 02

Automation and agent support

During analysis, measurement and form ranking are automated; the studio checks every output before delivery. The clinical decision is yours.

01

Measurement

From two photos, tooth proportion, midline, incisal display and gingival line are measured automatically.

02

Form proposal

Based on the measurements and facial proportions, 5 primary and 3 alternative forms are ranked from a library of 441, each with its rationale.

03

Preview and gate

The chosen form is fitted onto the patient's photo with guide lines; tooth count and contour must pass an automated check before anything is sent to you.

04

Approval and delivery

You review the preview on a single screen, approve or request a change; after approval the ExoCAD work package is prepared.

Limit: Atlas does not diagnose, prescribe treatment or promise clinical outcomes; the decision is the dentist's.

§ 04

Frequently asked questions

Q01

Which photos are required?

Two shots: a full-face smile shot and a retracted shot, in daylight or flat light. A phone camera is enough. You can add an intraoral scan if you have one. Patient consent must be obtained prior to work, with patient identity kept out of file names.

Q02

What do I get?

5 primary and 3 alternative form suggestions, each with a short reason; a guide-lined before/after preview with your chosen form (single file); after your approval, an ExoCAD work package.

Q03

I don't have ExoCAD. Can we still work together?

Yes. Form recommendations and the preview reach you without ExoCAD. An ExoCAD work package is prepared only if you move on to the arrangement step.

Q04

Where does patient data stay?

Photos are processed and stored on the studio's devices. For the preview, only an identity-free mouth crop is sent to the cloud image model; name, file name and identity details go nowhere. Write to us whenever you want them deleted. Details: Privacy and data note.

Q05

How do I find out the fee and turnaround?

Reach out via our contact email; turnaround and terms are given in our reply. We don't sell software; we prepare each case one by one.
