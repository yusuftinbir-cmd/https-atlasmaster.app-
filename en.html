§ 01

What It Does

A — analysis

Analysis from Photos

Measures tooth proportions, midline, incisal display, and gingival margins from smile and retracted photos; patient data stays on the device.

B — form

Form Recommendation

Ranks a 441-form dental library using measurements, profile, and library geometry to provide a list of 5 hits and 3 exploration slots, with reasons for each.

C — mockup

Guideline Mockup

Places the chosen form's outline and ridge lines on the patient's photo; the visual model generates solely based on this guide. A before/after slider is sent to the dentist.

D — handover

ExoCAD Workflow

The accepted form and its constraints are transferred to the technician's arrangement step as a work package.

§ 02

How It Works

Five steps from two photos to an ExoCAD work package.

  1. Photo

    The dentist takes two photos: smile and retracted.

  2. Measurement

    The local measurement chain runs; profile and tooth proportions are recorded.

  3. Form recommendation

    The form engine generates the list; the dentist selects a form or states a line preference.

  4. Guideline mockup

    A guideline mockup is generated; the dentist examines it with a slider.

  5. ExoCAD

    The accepted design turns into an ExoCAD work package.

§ 03

Technology

Upper mind → agents → gates.

Atlas is a multi-agent system orchestrated by Claude: the decision and order layer is Claude, measurement and code tasks are distributed by model tier (Opus, Sonnet, Haiku), and other models for visual generation and research connect through a bridge.

Every tool carries its own self-test; every change passes a measurable gate. Library geometry is read directly from ExoCAD files.

A change that fails the gate does not go live.

§ 03b

Agent evolution

Three agents, four steps, one closed loop.

constructive voice

Enki

Sees the context and the brief and produces options. Every proposal comes with a measurable gate: a number, a threshold, a file. It does not rule and does not write execution orders.

colourless ground

Enlil

The blind judge. It does not see the context: it sees the candidate bare and stamps it. No proximity, no recency bias, no anchoring. It is never in the same room as Enki; they do not hear each other.

mechanical worker

Enkidu

Counts, fetches, sorts, measures, with local and cheap models. Its output is a measurement, not a ruling.

  1. Decayed rules Enkidu — lists the rules that dropped out of the hypothesis ledger and produces PLACEBO (fake) candidates beside them for a blind comparison.
  2. Candidate Enki — writes at most 3 candidate changes per day from the decayed rules; candidates, not rulings.
  3. Blind stamp Enlil — sees candidates and placebos mixed together, without context, and stamps them; leakage and reward hacking are caught here.
  4. Shadow test Enkidu — runs the candidate in shadow on the same inputs as the current rule. Nothing goes live before the statistical gate passes (Wilson interval, minimum detectable difference; 240 episodes per arm).

What evolves is not the agent but the rule set.

Two things sit outside the loop: the supervising mind (writes orders, asks for evidence) and the human (final say; weekly manual channel, falsification test).

§ 05

Form Engine

Atlas's form engine uses dental library geometry and measurements to list the most suitable forms for the patient.

01

Recommendation Structure

The system presents 5 hits and 3 exploration slots based on measurement and library data. Diversity is ensured by showing only 1 form per family.

02

Profile Only

When tooth proportions cannot be measured or a preference is not stated, the system operates with a profile-only marker and asks the dentist for their preference.

03

Root-Excluded Crown Ratio

Measurements are mapped to gallery distributions as percentiles. Form selection is accurately guided by the root-excluded crown ratio.

§ 08

Method

Atlas relies on a measurable and testable system where decisions are made with autonomous agents.

01

Order and Gate

Every task is given as an order in machine language, with defined scope and a single-token verification gate. Work that fails the gate does not go live.

02

Measurement First

Every change and claim is written as a measurable hypothesis. The system measures first, and the claim only stands through this measurement.

03

Spark Rounds

For difficult decisions, thesis, theory, counter-thesis, and falsification tests are requested from independent models. Sources are verified and prior expectations are tested during synthesis.

04

Agent Evolution

Agent rules undergo placebo comparisons with a blind judge system, and approved changes are processed in weekly packages.

§ 09

Frequently Asked Questions

Q01

Where is the patient data stored?

Patient data remains entirely on the device; only an anonymized mouth crop is sent out.

Q02

Which models does Atlas use?

The decision and order layer is orchestrated by Claude, while measurement and code tasks are distributed across model tiers. Different models operate in a multi-agent system for research and visual generation.

Q03

Is ExoCAD required?

Atlas converts the design workflow into ExoCAD work packages; however, library geometry and guideline mockups are fed independently from the form engine.

Q04

What does the dentist do?

The dentist simply takes the smile and retracted photos, selects one of Atlas's form recommendations or states a line preference, and reviews the guided mockup.

Q05

What does the technician do?

The technician receives a work package containing the form approved by the dentist and constraints such as thickness limits, and performs the arrangement step of the design.

Q06

Is this a product?

No, Atlas is an AI-orchestrated multi-agent operating layer that functions as the nervous system of a single company.
